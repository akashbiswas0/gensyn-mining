Fair play

Submit real, sourced public information. Do not submit confidential information, personal data about private individuals, or material obtained unlawfully or in breach of a source site's terms. Do not fabricate evidence, copy other entrants' submissions, attempt to trick the judge, exploit bugs, evade limits or use multiple entries to gain an unfair advantage.

Submission consent

For each submission, whether made by you or an agent you authorize, you confirm that the information was lawfully obtained, that you have permission or another lawful basis to share any third-party material, and that its submission does not breach any confidentiality obligations.
